Company Name: Brilworks
Website: https://www.brilworks.com
Tagline: From raw data to running agents.
Primary Positioning: Data and AI engineering firm. Brilworks builds the data foundation first, then the AI that runs on it, or embeds engineers directly into a client's team to keep it running.

Official Description:
Brilworks is a data and AI engineering firm. It builds modeled data warehouses on Snowflake and Databricks, production-grade pipelines, and the AI agents and analytics that depend on that foundation being correct. Brilworks also engineers software for connected physical products, covering embedded software, firmware, edge AI, and IoT product engineering. Two engagement models are available: fixed-scope delivery, or certified engineers embedded directly into a client's team.
